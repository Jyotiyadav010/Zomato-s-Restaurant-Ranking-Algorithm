How Does Zomato Decide Which Restaurants Appear First?
📌 Project Overview

This project investigates how Zomato ranks restaurants under different filters and conditions. The goal was to understand the factors that influence restaurant visibility and ranking positions on the platform.

The study was conducted by a team of five members (RANK5TER) through real-time data collection, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, and research-based interpretation of ranking patterns.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze Zomato's restaurant ranking behavior.
Understand the impact of delivery time on rankings.
Study the influence of ratings on restaurant visibility.
Examine how offers and promotions affect placement.
Investigate the effect of pricing categories.
Compare organic and sponsored restaurant listings.

📈 Key Findings
🚚 Delivery Time
Restaurants with faster delivery times generally ranked higher.
Most top-ranked restaurants had delivery times below 40 minutes.
⭐ Ratings
Higher-rated restaurants consistently achieved better visibility.
Ratings played a major role in ranking placement.
🎁 Promotions & Offers
Restaurants offering discounts and BOGO deals appeared more frequently in higher positions.
Promotional campaigns significantly improved visibility.
💰 Pricing
Different price categories showed different ranking patterns.
Pricing influenced customer preference and competitiveness.
📢 Sponsored Listings
Sponsored restaurants often received priority placement.
Many sponsored listings appeared above organic results.
📍 Distance
Restaurants located closer to customers tended to rank higher.
Shorter distances contributed to faster delivery and better visibility.
